# Secure Wallet Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 29, 2026

## Overview
This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information Secure Wallet ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. 

## Data Collection
Secure Wallet is designed with your privacy in mind. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our servers.

- **Local Storage:** All data you input into the App (including passwords, credit card numbers, photos, voice notes, and scanned documents) is encrypted and stored locally on your device.
- **Third-Party Services:** We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking services that collect data from the App.

## Permissions
To provide its functionality, Secure Wallet may request the following permissions:
- **Camera:** Required for scanning barcodes or physical documents natively.
- **Microphone:** Required for recording audio payloads locally.
- **Face ID/Touch ID:** Used for secure and quick authentication to unlock your local database.
- **Photo Library:** Required if you wish to attach images or media from your device to your items.

These permissions are solely for enabling features within the app. Media accessed via these permissions is only saved locally within the app's encrypted sandbox.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
